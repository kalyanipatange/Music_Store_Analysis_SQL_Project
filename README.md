Some key insights from the analysis:
• Rock and Pop genres generated the highest sales

• A few customers contributed a major share of revenue

• Some countries showed higher music purchase activity than others

• Popular artists and albums played an important role in overall sales



Recommendations:

• Focus promotions on top-performing genres and artists

• Provide personalized recommendations to customers

• Build loyalty programs for repeat customers

• Increase marketing efforts in high-performing regions
